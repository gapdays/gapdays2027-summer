## Travel

Manchester Airport has direct flights from many European and international
destinations. Trains from the airport reach Manchester Piccadilly in about
20 minutes.

The university's Oxford Road campus is south of the city centre, 15-20 minutes
on foot from the stations Manchester Oxford Road and Manchester Piccadilly.
Frequent buses run along Oxford Road through the campus.

## Accommodation

Manchester has a wide range of hotels in the city centre, within walking
distance or a short bus ride of the campus. Recommendations will follow.
